Our program

Kettle Run High School · Nokesville, Virginia

Welcome to KR Swimming, established in 2008. We’re a program built on consistent effort, team-first support, and pride in representing our school the right way. We welcome both year-round and seasonal athletes who are committed to improving, encouraging their teammates, and bringing positive energy to the deck every day.

No matter your experience level, if you’re ready to work, learn, and contribute, there’s a place for you here. High school swimmers and year-round club swimmers train and compete together as one team.

How we run

What we value, and what we expect from every swimmer

What we value

  • Effort

    Consistent work at every practice, whatever your level. Improvement comes from showing up and putting in the work.

  • Attitude

    Positive energy on the deck. Being on time, prepared, and ready to learn.

  • Sportsmanship

    Respect for teammates, opponents, officials, and volunteers, before, during, and after every meet.

  • Team first

    Swimming looks individual, but we score, travel, and cheer as a team. Every swimmer counts.

Two kinds of athletes

Every swimmer is one or the other, and expectations follow from that.

VHSL-primary athlete

High school swimming is your main swimming

  • Aim to take part in everything: every practice and every meet.
  • If you have an absence or a conflict for any reason, tell the coach ahead of time. Otherwise it counts as unexcused and toward lettering.

USA Swimming athlete

You train with a club at least 6 hours a week

  • Keep training with your club. High school practice isn’t required.
  • Swim in as many team meets as you can, and tell the coach about any meet absence ahead of time.

Reporting an absence

Tell the coach before the practice or meet. Every absence is recorded.

Swimmers

  • Post in the team GroupMe chat
  • Send a GroupMe message that includes a parent
  • Email the coach with a parent copied

Swimmers can’t call or text the coach, or message one-on-one. Another adult is always included.

Parents

  • GroupMe (the team chat or a direct message)
  • Email
  • A call or a text

What counts as excused

A sick day from school, or a scheduled school event that conflicts with practice. Other situations are reviewed case by case, but they still need to be communicated as soon as possible.

On time and prepared
Practice: suit, goggles, cap, swim gear, and a water bottle, ready at the start time. Meets: at the pool (or school, for the bus) with all your gear by the arrival time. Being late is noted.
Positive attitude and sportsmanship
No bullying, no foul language, and nothing that brings others down. Issues are handled with a verbal warning, then a parent contact, then a KRHS administrator.
Follow school and FCPS rules
Nothing against the law, the FCPS Code of Student Conduct, or the FCPS Athletic Handbook. A suspension during the season means dismissal from the team and no letter.
Be safe
At the pool, in public areas, and in the parking lot. Student drivers follow Virginia’s passenger laws. Once at the pool, swimmers stay unless the coach says otherwise.
