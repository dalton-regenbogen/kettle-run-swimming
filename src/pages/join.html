How joining works

No one-day tryout

Instead of a one-day tryout, the first week of practice is an evaluation and onboarding week. Swimmers train with the team, so everyone gets a real sense of the team environment, expectations, and commitment. During that week the coach looks at each swimmer’s fitness, technique, effort, attitude, and sportsmanship—the qualities our program values most—so swimmers and families can make an informed decision before the season moves forward.

Costs and gear

What’s required, what’s provided, what’s optional

Fees
An activity fee and a team t-shirt fee. The school adds them to student accounts once the roster is final.
Provided by the team
Two KRHS swim caps, a team t-shirt, and a team jacket for the season—handed out at the first meet.
Team suit (optional)
Any plain suit works for meets—black is preferred—with no logos from other teams or programs. A durable, competition-style suit is best.
Spirit wear (optional)
KRHS swim gear for swimmers and families. Not required, and it has no effect on being on the team. Team apparel store ↗

Two ways to be on the team

For high school swimmers and for year-round club swimmers

VHSL-primary athlete

High school swimming is your main swimming

  • Attend all scheduled practices. If you’ll miss one, tell the coach before that day’s practice.
  • Compete in all team meets and support your teammates.
  • Let the coach know about any meet absence ahead of time.

USA Swimming athlete

You train with a club at least 6 hours a week

  • Keep training with your club—high school practice isn’t required.
  • Swim in as many team meets as you can, and come to team events to cheer on teammates.
  • Let the coach know about any meet absence ahead of time.

Practice basics

Getting there and what to bring

No school transportation to practice

Families arrange rides to and from WARF. Student drivers must follow Virginia’s passenger laws.

Practices are after school at WARF—see the practice schedule for days and times. Be on deck and ready to start on time with:

  • Swimsuit, goggles, and swim cap
  • A refillable water bottle
  • Any other swim gear you use

Common questions

Tap a question to see the answer

Does my swimmer need meet experience? +−

No. The first week of practice is about fitness, technique, effort, attitude, and sportsmanship—not past results.

I swim for a club. Can I still swim for Kettle Run? +−

Yes. If you train with your club at least 6 hours a week, you can join as a USA Swimming athlete: keep your club training, and swim in as many team meets as you can.

Do we have to buy a team suit? +−

No. Any plain suit works—black is preferred—as long as it has no other team’s logo. Caps, a t-shirt, and a jacket are provided.

How do we know the forms went through? +−

Families get an emailed sheet showing each swimmer’s form status. It updates once the school’s athletic trainer confirms each form.

What if my swimmer has to miss a practice or meet? +−

Let the coach know ahead of time—ideally the swimmer, but a parent is fine—by group chat, email, or text. Absences without notice count as unexcused.
