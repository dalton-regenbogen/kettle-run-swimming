Design lab · Typography, round 2

Clean, "Apple Sports" style

Apple Sports uses Apple's own font, SF Pro, which can only be used on Apple devices. Option 1 shows the real thing (on an iPhone or Mac). Options 2–4 are free fonts in the same spirit that look the same on every device. Option 5 is Archivo from round 1 for comparison.

All five use the same Apple-style treatment: heavy, tightly spaced headlines; title-case nav; small spaced-out labels; and tabular numbers so times and scores line up.
